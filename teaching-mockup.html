Teaching

Every course I teach starts from a question.

Graduate

SOCY 7703Spring 2021 – Fall 2024
4 semesters

What can a regression line tell us, and when does it mislead?

Multivariate Statistics

Ordinary least squares from the ground up: how it works, what it assumes, and how to check. Then linear probability and quantile models, missing data, survey weights, mediation, and a first look at panel data.

SOCY 7704Fall 2015 – Spring 2025
10 semesters

What if the outcome is yes or no, one of several choices, or a count?

Regression Models for Categorical Data

Logit and probit, multinomial and ordered logit, count models, and survival analysis, with maximum likelihood, missing data, and complex survey design along the way.

SOCY 7706Fall 2025

What do we learn by following the same people over time?

Longitudinal Data Analysis

Fixed-effects and mixed-effects models, duration models, sequence analysis, and time series, each read alongside published studies.

SOCY 7717Spring 2019

Will it happen, and when?

Event History Analysis and Sequence Analysis

Life tables, hazard models, competing risks, and time-varying covariates; then sequence analysis, for when the whole path matters. Students use the course to move their own projects forward.

GRADUATESpring 2017

Why do some problems come to be seen as medical?

Medical Sociology

The meanings of illness, who gets sick, how people seek help, and how doctors, nurses, and patients interact.
