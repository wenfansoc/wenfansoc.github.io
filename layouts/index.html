I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Sociology at Boston College, affiliated with the Sloan Research Network on Aging & Work. My research asks how social change, work environments, and family dynamics intersect to shape health and well-being over the life course and across social contexts.

I am currently working on three projects: an NSF-funded study of remote and hybrid work during the COVID-19 pandemic (with Phyllis Moen); a project on the worldwide impacts of four-day workweeks (with Juliet Schor, Orla Kelly, and Phyllis Moen); and a study of pandemic-precipitated social change and mental health in China (funded by CIHR; PI: Yue Qian). I received my Ph.D. in Sociology and M.S. in Statistics from the University of Minnesota.

Work Time Reduction Via a Four-day Workweek Finds Improvements in Workers' Well-being
Nature Human Behaviour · with J. Schor, O. Kelly & G. Gu
Drawing on trials across organizations in multiple countries, we find that moving to a four-day week—with no loss of pay—improves workers' well-being, reducing burnout and lifting mental and physical health.

Demography · with Y. Qian

Journal of Health and Social Behavior · with P. Moen
