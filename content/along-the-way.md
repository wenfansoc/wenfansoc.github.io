Each of the readings below changed something in me, if only a little, at the time I read it, and they appear in the order I read them. Several of them speak to a question behind my research on [the meaning of work](/research/#meaning): what are people looking for when they look for meaning in their lives and work? For years, I read mostly to learn how to do my work well. Then the question became personal, when I began to wonder what my own work was for. Looking for an answer led me to many of the books below, and later to Vipassana meditation, which trains attention on bodily sensation rather than on thoughts, feelings, or stories. Meditation has not given me a clear answer, but it has set me on the way toward one. Along the way, I have come to see how much of experience goes unspoken, and how much of it we do not know, or do not know that we do not know. The same question runs through a new seminar I will teach in spring 2027, [*A Self of One's Own*](/teaching/#a-self-of-ones-own).
